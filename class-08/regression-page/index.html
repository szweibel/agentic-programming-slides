How to read this: positive differences mean more days to close; negative differences mean fewer. Adding complaint type estimates a borough difference while holding complaint type constant. It does not establish that a borough causes faster or slower closure. Closure is an administrative outcome, not a measure of whether a resident’s problem was resolved.

Method: ordinary least squares with an intercept and categorical borough indicators, optionally adding categorical complaint-type indicators (no interactions). All rows, including “Other,” enter both fits. The plot always shows observed times and unadjusted averages. Embedded data; no network connection needed.
