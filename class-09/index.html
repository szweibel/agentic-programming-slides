I'm a master's student in data analysis. I don't have a research project, and I'm not sure what I'd use you for. Interview me, one question at a time, about my work, my courses, and what I'm interested in. After a few questions, propose three projects I could build with you in about six weeks.

From an interest to a question

  • These steps come from The Craft of Research, by Wayne Booth, Gregory Colomb and Joseph Williams

Questions to ask about a topic

Kind of questionFor the student’s topic
HistoryHow have visits from each zip code changed over the years?
StructureWhich of the five pantries get most of the visits?
ComparisonDo the five pantries draw visitors from different zip codes?
Turned aroundWhich zip codes have farmers markets and still send many visitors?
What ifWhat if a market opened next to the busiest pantry?
  • The book prefers how and why questions to who, what, when and where

The three-part sentence

I am studying pantry visits and farmers markets in Bronx zip codes

because I want to find out whether zip codes with more pantry visits have fewer markets

so that I can help the board understand which neighborhoods a market partnership would reach

  • Then ask “So what?” until the answer names something the reader cares about

Choosing one idea

  • Can you check the output yourself?
  • Are you allowed to use the data?
  • Can it be done in six weeks?
  • Will anyone use it, even just you?

The project proposal

PartThe student’s proposal
Question or taskMake the monthly pantry report in an hour instead of two days
DataMonthly visit counts from five pantries, by zip code, with no names
Course techniquesSaved versions (Class 4), a skill for the monthly run (Class 6), checking each row against its source (Class 7)
Finished thingA tool that turns the five spreadsheets into the board report and charts
How you’ll check itRun it on three past months and compare with the reports made by hand

Codex’s criticism of the proposal

The student

I picked the report builder. Here is my proposal. Tell me what’s wrong or missing in it. Don’t rewrite it for me.

Codex

The main gap is that your finished tool starts with five spreadsheets, but your actual work also includes paper sign-in sheets. You need to define whether the one-hour target includes entering those counts.

Codex also asked

  • Do all five pantries use the same columns and count visits the same way?
  • Could the hand-made reports have errors too?
  • How long does the whole monthly run take?
